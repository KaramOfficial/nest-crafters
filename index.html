Which areas do you serve? Call +1 (236) 880-5484

We’re based in Surrey, British Columbia, and serve Surrey, Vancouver and the wider Lower Mainland. Send us your project location when you get in touch.

Do you do renovations or just cabinets?

We offer kitchen renovations as well as custom cabinetry. Our work spans concept development, design and installation. Tell us what you’re planning so we can discuss the right scope for your project.

Can you work with modern and classic styles?

Yes. Our kitchen cabinetry is custom-designed to suit both modern and classic preferences. Share the colours, materials or inspiration you’re drawn to, and we can use those ideas to start the conversation.

Do you take on commercial projects?

Yes. Alongside our residential work, we offer custom cabinetry and millwork for retail stores and other commercial interiors. Message us about your business space and what you need it to do.

How do I get a quote?

Message Nest Crafters on Facebook to request your free quote and consultation. A short description of your project, your location and any photos or ideas you have are a helpful place to start.
