About us

Nest Crafters

We create custom-designed cabinets that combine style, functionality and quality — from sleek modern kitchens to timeless classics, and from closets and built-ins to retail and commercial millwork across Surrey, Vancouver & the Lower Mainland.

Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you serve?

Surrey, Vancouver and the wider Lower Mainland, BC. Include your project location when you message us.

Do you do renovations or just cabinets?

Both — kitchen renovations as well as custom cabinetry, from concept and design through installation.

Can you work with modern and classic styles?

Yes. Every project is custom-designed, so share the colours, materials or inspiration you're drawn to.

Do you take on commercial projects?

Yes — custom cabinetry and millwork for retail stores and other commercial interiors.

How do I get a quote?

Message @nestcraftersltd on Instagram or find us on Facebook. Quotes and consultations are free.
